# MM640g alebo MM630g

-   Ak ho ešte nemáte, stiahnite si [600SeriesAndroidUploader](https://pazaan.github.io/600SeriesAndroidUploader/) a postupujte podľa pokynov na stránke [Nightscout](https://nightscout.github.io/uploader/setup/?h=uploader#medtronic-600-series-with-uploader).
-   V 600 Series Uploader choďte do Settings > Send to xDrip a zvoľte ON (zaškrtnite).

![600 series uploader](../images/600Uploader.png)

-   Vyberte MM640g v [**Konfigurácia → Zdroj glykémie**](#Config-Builder-bg-source).
